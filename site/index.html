Contribution graph

One block of weeks per year. Hovering a day cell lists the films watched that day, the year label breaks the films down by release decade, and the headline figures each open a distribution of their own.

Year in review

1200×630, the Open Graph default, so a card works as a social preview as it stands. Headline figures on the left, top rated films on the right with poster art, runtime and the community rating.

Month in review

The same card narrowed to a single month. The files are named by how recent the month is, not by its date, so an embed keeps working when the month turns over and old cards do not pile up.

Profile card

Not tied to a year. The headline is the all-time films watched taken from the profile page; the right column shows the favourites pinned on it.

Recent diary

The last ten entries, straight out of the JSON export. ↻ marks a rewatch, ♥ a like.
